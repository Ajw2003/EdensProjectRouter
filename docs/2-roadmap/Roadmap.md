# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Approved design (10%)

**Contains:** a design page with the stack, layout and onboarding choices.
**Acceptance:** Aj replies "Design approved: router prototype" with their picks.

## Milestone 2 — Working prototype on Windows (70%)

**Contains:** an installable EdensRouter-Setup.exe with four account slots out of the box and an Add account button for any number more, session discovery per account, sessions grouped and colour coded by project, a way to start or resume a session under a chosen account, built-in onboarding.
**Acceptance:** Aj starts it on their Windows PC with one double-click, sees their real sessions from all four accounts grouped by project, and starts a session under a chosen account from it.

## Milestone 3 — Visual view (proposed, 45%)

**Contains:** a Visual view beside the terminal, drawn from the session transcript, with question cards, action cards with a Why line, inline pages and pictures, a Now strip, a Needs-you list and calm scrolling.
**Acceptance:** in a live session, a question Claude asks shows as a card, tapping an answer moves the terminal on, and a page Claude writes shows in the conversation.

## Milestone 4 — Account handoff (proposed, 55%)

**Contains:** an account router that opens each project on its last account and moves a conversation to another account in one tap.
**Acceptance:** a session started on Main resumes on Work with its earlier messages visible.

## Milestone 5 — Phone on home Wi-Fi (proposed, 75%)

**Contains:** an Android APK that pairs with the PC by QR code and has the same Terminal and Visual views, with a notification when a session needs Aj.
**Acceptance:** Aj answers a question in the Visual view on the phone and sees the PC's terminal move on.

## Milestone 6 — Phone away from home (proposed, 85%)

**Acceptance:** the Milestone 5 (phone on home Wi-Fi) test passes with the phone on mobile data.

Milestones 3 to 6 come from the plan page `docs/plans/companion-app.html`, waiting for Aj's approval.

## Milestone 7 — Daily use (100%)

**Contains:** fixes from Aj's own use.
**Acceptance:** Aj has used it for a week of normal work and says it is done. The exact list is TODO until milestone 2 has been used.
